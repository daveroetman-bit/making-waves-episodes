## Summary

Recorded in mid-March 2026, after the main run of the legislative session and before Gov. Larry Rhoden signed the three bills Bartscher discusses. Guest Dale Bartscher, Associate Director and lobbyist, South Dakota Right to Life; Republican candidate for the South Dakota House, District 35 (Rapid City, Rapid Valley, Box Elder and Ellsworth Air Force Base) "As Conservatives, we are not far-right, we are not far-wrong, we are just right. We are right in prioritizing the education of our children, whether it is public school, private school or homeschool. These and other Conservative issues remain the core values of the Republican Party. And I support them and I will stand with them." - Dale Bartscher Update (October 2026) Gov. Larry Rhoden signed all three bills on March 20, 2026, at the Alpha Center, a crisis pregnancy center in Sioux Falls, and they took effect July 1. HB 1274 makes it a felony to dispense, distribute or advertise abortion pills and related items for an unlawful abortion. HB 1274 is now tied up in federal court. Mayday Health and former Democratic legislator Nancy Turbak Berry sued Rhoden and Attorney General Marty Jackley in May. On July 17, U.S. District Judge Camela Theeler in Rapid City granted a preliminary injunction that blocks the state from enforcing the law's advertising ban against them while the case goes on. The 8th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals denied the state's request to overturn that order (reported Oct. 2). Jackley's office said in July that the case will still go to trial. For HB 1313, the state Department of Education now lists approved videos for the new requirement, including Britannica's "How a Human Embryo Develops into a Fetus" and Live Action's "Baby Olivia." On the campaign, Bartscher finished first in the June 2 Republican primary for District 35's two House seats with 1,241 votes (33.8%), ahead of Jason Fleming (1,205), Jace West (744) and Emmett Reistroffer (484). National Right to Life endorsed him in May. He and Fleming face Democrat Ken Jeakins on Nov. 3; no incumbent is on the ballot.
