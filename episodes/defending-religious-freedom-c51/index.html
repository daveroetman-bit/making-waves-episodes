Show notes

Show notes

Nathan Shackelford of First Liberty Institute joins Dave Roetman to talk about defending religious freedom in America: First Liberty's volunteer-attorney model, its Supreme Court wins for school choice in Maine and for Coach Joe Kennedy, the end of the Lemon test, and the Groff v. DeJoy Sabbath case.

Recorded in May 2023. Update: on June 29, 2023, the Supreme Court ruled unanimously for Gerald Groff in Groff v. DeJoy.
