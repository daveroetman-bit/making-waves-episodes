Show notes

Show notes

Leah Anderson is the Minnehaha County Auditor. She earned her accounting degree at the University of South Dakota in 1993 and worked in public accounting, doing financial audits and some tax work, before spending more than 20 years doing the accounting for small businesses in Sioux Falls. She was elected auditor in 2022 and took office in 2023. The auditor's office runs elections in South Dakota's largest county and also handles the county's budget, accounting and payroll, the county commission's minutes, and sales of tax-deed property.

Recorded in early January 2024 and first published Jan. 4, 2024. The "upcoming" ES&S training and the April 9 combined city and school election she mentions were in 2024.

Update (Oct. 2026): Anderson is running for a second term. She won the June 2, 2026 Republican primary over Samuel McCarty of Hartford, 12,814 votes to 4,725 (about 73%), and faces Democrat Curt Deedrich in the Nov. 3, 2026 general election, the only contested race for an incumbent county office on Minnehaha County's ballot. In her 2026 campaign she points to post-election audits, the release of cast vote records, limiting local voting to true South Dakota residents, and changes to absentee ballot processing so the county can report results on time when it has 30,000 or more absentee ballots.
