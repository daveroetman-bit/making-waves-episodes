Show notes

Show notes

Dan Goeller, founder and executive director of Harmony South Dakota and creator of the YouTube channel Leninists on the Prairie, joins Dave Roetman again, this time to define "woke" and show where he sees it in South Dakota. He calls woke "a compliance structure" built on an oppressor-and-victim narrative. Goeller walks through how he says the agenda reaches Sioux Falls: national foundations and federal grants fund local nonprofits, and local charities and funders attach DEI and equity requirements to their money. He also takes on the Chamber of Commerce's lobbying, corporate ESG giving, and the Target and Bud Light boycotts, and he gives listeners his answer: don't comply, don't give, get informed, and share what you learn. This episode follows Dan's first Making Waves conversation, "The March Through Institutions."

Recorded in fall 2023.

Update (Oct. 2026): Since this conversation, Target said in January 2025 that it was ending its three-year DEI goals and scaling back other diversity programs. In July 2025 Goeller asked the Minnehaha County Commission to stop payroll deductions for the Sioux Empire United Way over its DEI criteria. In August 2025 a majority of commissioners said they'd keep the option. South Dakota Voices for Peace, one of the groups Goeller criticizes here, says in its 2025 impact report that it received a 2025 Bush Foundation Innovation Grant of $995,900 over two years. Goeller is still Harmony South Dakota's executive director and still runs Leninists on the Prairie. (Sources are linked below.)
