Show notes

Show notes

Brandei Schaefbauer is a Republican state representative from Aberdeen, in District 3. She grew up in Mobridge, earned a surgical technology degree at Presentation College, worked for orthopedic doctors, and raised four sons before running for the House in 2022. In this conversation, recorded days after she won a second term, she talks about what the 2024 ballot results mean for the 2025 session, her "words matter" approach to writing laws, and the issues she planned to work on.

Recorded in November 2024 and first published Nov. 10, 2024. Right after the interview was recorded, House Republicans elected her one of five majority whips for the 2025-2026 term.

Update (Oct. 2026): The 2024 results she discussed were certified: voters rejected Amendment G (abortion) 58.6% to 41.4%, IM 28 (food sales tax) 69% to 31%, IM 29 (recreational marijuana) 56% to 44%, and Referred Law 21 (the carbon pipeline law) 59% to 41%, and they approved Amendment F (Medicaid work requirements) 56% to 44%. Rep. Schaefbauer served as a House majority whip in the 2025 and 2026 sessions. On March 12, 2026, she announced she would not seek reelection. In the June 2, 2026 Republican primary for the two District 3 House seats, voters nominated Spencer Sommers and Tim Hanigan, and Democrat Erin Rudner won her party's primary. The District 3 House race is on the Nov. 3, 2026 general election ballot. (Sources are linked below.)
