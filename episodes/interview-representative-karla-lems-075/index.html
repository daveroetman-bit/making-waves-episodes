Show notes

Representative Karla Lems, a first-term South Dakota House member from District 16, discusses her eminent domain bill, 1133, and why she says Summit Carbon Solutions and Navigator should not take South Dakota land for carbon pipelines. She also covers a UCC update she says lays groundwork for a central bank digital currency, and HB 1080, which would ban puberty blockers, cross-sex hormones and gender-transition surgeries for minors.
