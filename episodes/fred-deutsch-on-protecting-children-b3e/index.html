Show notes

Representative Fred Deutsch of District 4 talks about HB 1080, Rep. Bethany Soye's bill, now law, which protects children from gender-transition surgeries, puberty blockers and cross-sex hormones. He also raises judges changing sex on birth certificates and a Minnesota proposal to let South Dakota children cross the border for those procedures, and warns about an outside-funded measure to put abortion in the state constitution.
