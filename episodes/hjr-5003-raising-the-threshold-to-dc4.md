## Summary

Show notes Recorded in January 2025, during Rep. John Hughes's first weeks in the South Dakota House, just after the House State Affairs Committee passed HJR 5003. Guest Rep. John Hughes (R-Sioux Falls), South Dakota House District 13, attorney and prime sponsor of HJR 5003 Update (October 2026) HJR 5003 passed the House 61-5 on Jan. 22, 2025, the day after this episode posted, and the Senate 29-4 on March 10, 2025. Sen. Sue Peterson, also of District 13, was the Senate prime sponsor. A joint resolution doesn't need the governor's signature, so it went straight to the Secretary of State and is on the Nov. 3, 2026 general election ballot as Constitutional Amendment L. Amendment L itself needs only a simple majority to pass. If it passes, every future amendment to the South Dakota Constitution, whether it is put on the ballot by citizens or by the Legislature, will need 60% of the votes cast on the question. Initiated measures and referred laws will still pass with a simple majority, as Rep. Hughes says here. Voters turned down earlier attempts to raise the bar in 2018 (Amendment X, 55%) and in the June 2022 primary (Amendment C, 60% for measures that raise taxes or spend $10 million or more). Rep. Hughes came back on Making Waves in September 2026 to talk about Amendment L.
