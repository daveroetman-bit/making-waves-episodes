Show notes

Sen. Mykala Voita (R-Bonesteel), District 21, a first-term member of the Joint Committee on Appropriations, joins Dave Roetman ahead of the 2026 session for an overview of the state budget. Voita notes that 43% of South Dakota’s budget is federal money, down from a COVID-era spike, and warns that one-time federal dollars have created ongoing costs the state may now have to carry. She explains that she voted against the new men’s prison mainly because of its ongoing expenses, asks what is being done right now for inmates in the state’s care, and praises Burl Cain’s presentation on prison rehabilitation. On agriculture, she discusses President Trump’s comments on Argentine beef, the “Big Four” meatpackers and his call for a Justice Department investigation, the Packers and Stockyards Act and buying beef straight from the rancher, calls cow-calf operators the last line of defense in national food security, and closes by urging leaders to stay grounded in faith.

Topics: state budget, federal funding, Appropriations Committee, one-time COVID funds, new men’s prison, prison rehabilitation, recidivism, Burl Cain, Argentine beef imports, meatpacker concentration, DOJ meatpacker investigation, Packers and Stockyards Act, cow-calf operators, faith in leadership

“As of today, President Trump is finally hearing the truth. He is hearing that our cattle market is controlled by, we call them the Big Four. They’re mostly foreign-owned packers that control a very large majority of our whole beef market supply.” - Sen. Mykala Voita, District 21
