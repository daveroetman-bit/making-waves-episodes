Show notes

Representative Chris Karr, Chair of the House Appropriations Committee, provides an inside look at South Dakota’s budget surplus and the sales tax reduction bill that made it to the Governor’s desk. He also talks about the possibility of a budget veto, gives additional detail on the lewd and lascivious bill that was killed on the House floor, and discusses why he believes the DUI bill he sponsored would be extremely positive for the state.

The DUI Court program has an 80% success rate. That’s higher than any other program we were able to find. — Rep Chris Karr
