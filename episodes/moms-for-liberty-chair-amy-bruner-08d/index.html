Show notes

Amy Bruner, chair of the Minnehaha County chapter of Moms for Liberty, describes the parental-rights group and its three South Dakota chapters. She talks about sexually explicit books she and other parents found in school libraries across South Dakota, and about working with her local district, which put up a web page so parents can review materials. She urges parents to have civil, informed conversations with schools.
