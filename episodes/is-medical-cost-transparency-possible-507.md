## Summary

Show notes Recorded in January 2025 at Dunn Brothers Coffee, before the bill had a number. Guest Aaron Levisay, Sioux Falls, a 29-year military veteran who has worked on South Dakota's social studies standards and with Convention of States Action Update (October 2026) The bill Aaron previewed here became House Bill 1102 in the 2025 session, introduced by Rep. Aaron Aylward. It would have required insurers and third-party administrators to send medical, dental and pharmacy claims data to the state Division of Insurance and set up a health care data system. House Commerce and Energy tabled it 12-0 on Feb. 7, 2025, and no similar bill passed in 2026. As of mid-2026, South Dakota still has no all-payer claims database and no public, state-run price lookup. On the federal side, President Trump signed an executive order on Feb. 25, 2025, directing agencies to enforce and strengthen the hospital and insurer price transparency rules that date back to his first term. Links New Hampshire HealthCost, the state medical and dental price lookup Aaron describes: https://nhhealthcost.nh.gov/ APCD Council: https://www.apcdcouncil.org/ HB 1102 (2025), South Dakota Legislature: https://sdlegislature.gov/Session/Bill/25440 Patient Rights Advocate, Seventh Semi-Annual Hospital Price Transparency Report (November 2024): https://www.patientrightsadvocate.org/seventh-semi-annual-hospital-price-transparency-report-november-2024 Search any U.S. hospital's price file: https://hospitalpricingfiles.org/ Executive order on health care price transparency (White House, Feb. 25, 2025): https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/making-america-healthy-again-by-empowering-patients-with-clear-accurate-and-actionable-healthcare-pricing-information/
