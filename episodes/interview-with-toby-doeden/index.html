Show notes

Show notes

Published May 13, 2025. Toby Doeden officially launched his campaign for governor two weeks later, on May 28, 2025, at the Wylie Park Pavilion in Aberdeen, the event he talks about in this episode.

Guest

Toby Doeden, Aberdeen businessman (owner of Aberdeen Chrysler Center, Redfield Ford and other businesses and real estate), raised in Groton; Republican candidate for governor of South Dakota in 2026

Background

At his May 28, 2025, launch, Doeden pledged to eliminate property taxes, create a "South Dakota Department of Government Efficiency," audit every state agency and release every line item of state spending, and turn down the governor's salary.

Attorney General Marty Jackley, whose budget Doeden mentions, was on Making Waves on April 26, 2025.

Update (October 2026)

The primary: In the June 2, 2026, Republican primary for governor, Doeden finished first with 31%, ahead of Gov. Larry Rhoden (25%), U.S. Rep. Dusty Johnson (23%) and House Speaker Jon Hansen (21%). Nobody reached 35%, so for the first time under a 1985 state law, the race went to a top-two runoff.

The runoff: Gov. Rhoden won the July 28, 2026, runoff. Official results: Rhoden 83,590 votes, Doeden 34,435 (about 71% to 29%). Doeden carried only Fall River and Todd counties. He called Rhoden to concede that night, offered his "full-throated support" against Democrat Dan Ahlers, and said he was going back to running his companies.

Since then: President Trump endorsed Rhoden on Aug. 27, 2026. A final campaign finance report filed in October 2026 shows Doeden put about $5.3 million of his own money into the campaign. The general election between Rhoden and Ahlers is Nov. 3, 2026.
