## Summary

Representative Karla Lems of District 16 walks through the 2023 South Dakota tax-cut fights: Governor Noem's food sales tax cut, which was killed; Chris Karr's HB 1137 to cut the state sales tax from 4.5% to 4.2%, amended in the Senate with a two-year sunset; and Rep. Trish Ladner's property tax cut. She also talks about the budget surplus and raising Medicaid reimbursement for struggling nursing homes.
