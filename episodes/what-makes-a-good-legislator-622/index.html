Show notes

Show notes

Brent "B.R." Hoffman is a Republican who represented District 9 (western Sioux Falls, Hartford and Wall Lake) in the South Dakota Senate for two sessions, 2023 and 2024. He is an Air Force veteran, a writer and a former Sioux City city council member and school board member. He did not seek re-election in 2024, and this conversation was recorded in November 2024, after the election and before the 2025 session. The term-limits and school-safety bills he mentions did not pass. The two ballot measures Dave brings up were both defeated on Nov. 5, 2024: Amendment H (top-two open primaries) failed 65.6% to 34.4%, and Referred Law 21 (the carbon dioxide pipeline siting law) failed 59.4% to 40.6%.

Update (Oct. 2026): Hoffman left the Senate in January 2025 and is now running for the Minnehaha County Commission. He led the six-way Republican primary for three at-large seats on June 2, 2026 with 7,396 votes, ahead of incumbent Jen Bleyenberg (7,005) and Rick Bonander (6,300). The three Republicans face Democrat Kenny Anderson Jr. and independent Curt Soehl on Nov. 3, 2026, and the top three vote-getters win four-year terms. "I hope to reshape our county government, making it more open and careful with tax dollars," Hoffman told Sioux Falls Live. (Sources are linked below.)
