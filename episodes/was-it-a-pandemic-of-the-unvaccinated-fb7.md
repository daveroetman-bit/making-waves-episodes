## Summary

Show notes Bobbi Andera joins Dave Roetman to explain why she doesn't believe COVID-19 was a "pandemic of the unvaccinated." She covers the 14-day rule for counting hospital patients as vaccinated, the Diamond Princess cruise ship outbreak, what PCR testing can and can't tell you, and how COVID diagnosis codes and CMS reimbursement shaped hospital admissions. Andera has a master's degree and a bachelor's degree in medical technology and is certified through AMT as a medical laboratory scientist. She served in the military as a medical laboratory journeyman, later worked at Sanford Health, and runs her own laboratory consulting company. First published July 3, 2023, before she was elected to the Legislature. Update (Oct. 2026): Andera was elected to the South Dakota House from District 10 (Sioux Falls) in November 2024 and took office in January 2025. Her 2025 bill on conscience exemptions from certain vaccinations (HB 1223) failed on the House floor, 34-36. In 2026 her bill letting pharmacists dispense ivermectin and hydroxychloroquine under a written protocol (HB 1068) failed in the House 28-40, and her bill barring employers from requiring "genetic-based" vaccinations such as mRNA shots (HB 1163) failed 32-35. She lost the June 2, 2026 Republican primary. The Montana blood-donation bill she mentions, HB 645, had already been tabled in committee 19-1 in February 2023 and never became law.
