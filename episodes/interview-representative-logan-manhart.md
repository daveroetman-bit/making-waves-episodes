## Summary

Show notes Published May 16, 2025, after Logan Manhart's first session in the South Dakota House (the 100th legislative session, 2025). Guest Rep. Logan Manhart, R-Aberdeen, District 1 (Brown, Day, Marshall and Roberts counties); elected in 2024; construction and real estate Background HB 1052 (2025), the win Manhart talks about, bans the use of eminent domain for carbon dioxide pipelines. Gov. Larry Rhoden signed it on March 6, 2025. Toby Doeden, the friend Manhart says he encouraged to run for governor, was on Making Waves on May 13, 2025, and launched his campaign in Aberdeen on May 28, 2025. Update (October 2026) 2026 session: Manhart was the prime House sponsor of HB 1155, to let the state invest in Bitcoin. The House Commerce and Energy Committee killed it on Feb. 6, 2026 (deferred to the 41st day, 10-3). After the session he told Dakota Broadcasting he was not a fan of the property tax relief law lawmakers passed, which raised or added sales taxes to offset lower property taxes, and called the tax increases regressive. The primary: In the June 2, 2026, Republican primary for the two District 1 House seats, Manhart finished third with 1,398 votes (22.3%), behind Rep. Nick Fosness (1,855) and Daniel Kjos (1,664). He was one of 16 incumbent lawmakers who lost their primaries and one of five Freedom Caucus members who lost, according to South Dakota Searchlight. Fosness and Kjos face Democrat Courtney Enfield on Nov. 3, 2026. Manhart's term ends in January 2027. The governor's race: Toby Doeden finished first in the June primary but lost the July 28, 2026, runoff to Gov. Rhoden, 83,590 votes to 34,435.
