## Summary

Recorded in January 2026, in the first week of South Dakota's 2026 legislative session, a couple of days after Gov. Larry Rhoden's Jan. 15 executive order on the Future Fund. Guest - Rep. Marty Overweg (R-New Holland), District 21, Assistant House Majority Leader; co-chair of the interim Government Operations and Audit Committee (GOAC) Update (October 2026) SB 1, the GOAC bill to put Future Fund guardrails in state law, died in Senate State Affairs. The committee split 4-4 on Jan. 16, then voted 5-4 on Jan. 26 to defer it to the 41st day. The rules Gov. Rhoden set in Executive Order 2026-03 (reimbursement-only grants, signed agreements, posting awards on OpenSD and twice-a-year reports to the appropriations committees) stay in place, but only as an executive order. HB 1105, Overweg's bipartisan bill with Rep. Erik Muckey to block deed restrictions and non-competes that keep former health care buildings from being used for care in underserved areas, passed the House 45-20 on Feb. 11. Sanford, Avera and Monument Health opposed it, and Senate Health and Human Services tabled it 5-0 on Feb. 25. Corsica's nursing home closed Nov. 30, 2025. In October 2026, the Corsica Development Corporation's task force and Douglas County Memorial Hospital announced a deal to reopen the building as Prairie Winds Care Center, aiming for mid-2027 pending state and federal approval. Overweg won renomination in the June 2 Republican primary and is on the Nov. 3 ballot for District 21's two House seats. Links - Rep.
