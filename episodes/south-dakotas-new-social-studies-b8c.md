## Summary

Aaron Levisay, a member of South Dakota's social studies standards revision committee, discusses the standards finished in August 2022. He says they are comprehensive in history and geography, with Oceti content about 10 percent of the document and tribal educators, including Rep. Tamara St. John, on the committee. He answers criticism about age appropriateness and explains the grade-by-grade design and teacher training funds.
