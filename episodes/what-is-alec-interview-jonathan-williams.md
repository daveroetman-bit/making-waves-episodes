Guest Jonathan Williams, President and Chief Economist, American Legislative Exchange Council (ALEC); co-author of Rich States, Poor States with Dr. Arthur Laffer and Stephen Moore Update (October 2026) The 2026 Rich States, Poor States (19th edition, April 2026) ranks South Dakota 9th for economic outlook, down one spot from 8th in 2025, and 19th for economic performance. South Dakota's sales tax burden ranked 41st, and its property tax burden ranked 17th. On March 12, 2026, Gov. Larry Rhoden signed SB 96 and SB 245, which he called the largest property tax cut in state history. SB 96 lets counties add up to a half-cent sales tax to lower owner-occupied property taxes. SB 245 moves about $56 million into a new homeowner property tax reduction fund. Starting in July 2027 it also dedicates three-tenths of a percent of the state sales tax to that fund. He also signed HB 1258, which requires property tax bills to point taxpayers to a state property tax information website. On Oct. 5, 2026, ALEC announced that its board unanimously approved Jonathan Williams as ALEC's next Chief Executive Officer, starting Jan. 1, 2027. He'll replace Lisa B.
