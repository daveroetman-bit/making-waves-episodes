## Summary

Representative Jon Hansen of District 25 talks about education choice in South Dakota, including the HB 1234 voucher proposal and Governor Noem's SB 100 scholarship for foster children, and why he says the public school lobby fought them. He also discusses HB 1217 on taxpayer-funded lewd events, and warns about a proposed amendment he says would legalize abortion in South Dakota through nine months of pregnancy.
