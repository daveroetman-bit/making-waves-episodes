## Summary

Representatives Karla Lems and John Sjaarda explain the Summit Carbon Solutions and Navigator carbon-dioxide pipelines proposed across South Dakota, and why they say private companies should not use eminent domain to take land. Lems covers federal 45Q tax credits and pipeline safety. Sjaarda argues the pipelines' market is going away, and that private eminent domain here could later be used for solar and wind projects.
