## Summary

Representative Aaron Aylward of District 6, chair of the South Dakota Freedom Caucus, describes his ESG bills, HB 1207 and 1208, including an economic boycott bill aimed at banks and businesses that contract with the state; both died in committee. He also talks about his anti-red flag bill and his Defend the Guard bill, which would require a congressional declaration of war before the South Dakota National Guard is deployed.
