## Summary

Show notes Recorded in February 2025, in the middle of the legislative session. Guest Austin Goss, political reporter for The Dakota Scout: https://dakotascout.com/ Bills discussed SB 60, expand the access and investigatory authority of the state auditor: https://sdlegislature.gov/Session/Bill/25667 Attorney General Marty Jackley's testimony on SB 60 (SDPB): https://sdpb.sd.gov/sdpbpodcast/2025/sst09.mp3#t=244 HJR 5007, a constitutional amendment on state accounting and spending oversight: https://sdlegislature.gov/Session/Bill/25861 HB 1052, prohibit eminent domain for a pipeline that carries carbon oxide: https://sdlegislature.gov/Session/Bill/25581 Update (October 2026) SB 60 became law in 2025, giving the state auditor access to every agency's financial records and requiring the auditor to report improper conduct to the auditor general and the attorney general. HJR 5007 was tabled 9-1 in House State Affairs and did not reach the ballot. HB 1052 was signed on March 6, 2025, so a carbon dioxide pipeline can no longer use eminent domain in South Dakota. Lawmakers rejected the Lincoln County prison in February 2025. After Governor Rhoden's Project Prison Reset task force, a Sept. 23, 2025 special session approved a $650 million, 1,500-bed men's prison in northeast Sioux Falls instead. Links HB 1052 as signed (2025 Session Law Chapter 190): https://sdlegislature.gov/Statutes/Session_Laws/Chapter/8216 HJR 5007 House State Affairs vote: https://sdlegislature.gov/Session/Vote/81716 South Dakota Searchlight on the Sioux Falls prison vote: https://southdakotasearchlight.com/2025/09/23/south-dakota-lawmakers-approve-650-million-prison-construction-project-in-sioux-falls/
