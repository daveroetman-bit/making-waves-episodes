Show notes

Show notes

Chris Larson is a Sioux Falls entrepreneur who started Comfort King, a mattress factory with stores in Sioux Falls and Fargo, with his wife Margo in 1994. In August 2024 he became chair of VoteNoSD, a statewide ballot question committee whose "Vote No in NOvember" campaign urged a no vote on Amendment H, Amendment G, Referred Law 21 and Initiated Measure 29. In this conversation he also says he was voting no on IM 28, the sales tax measure, which was not part of the campaign.

Recorded in October 2024 and first published Oct. 11, 2024, ahead of that year's general election.

Update (Oct. 2026): All four measures the campaign targeted were defeated on Nov. 5, 2024. Amendment H (top-two primaries) failed 34% to 66%, Amendment G (abortion) failed 41% to 59%, Referred Law 21 failed 41% to 59%, which repealed SB 201, and IM 29 (recreational marijuana) failed 44% to 56%. IM 28, the sales tax measure Larson also opposed, failed 31% to 69%. In 2025 the Legislature went further on pipelines: House Bill 1052, which bars the use of eminent domain for a pipeline whose main purpose is carrying carbon oxide, passed the House 49-19 and the Senate 23-12, and Gov. Larry Rhoden signed it on March 6, 2025. (Sources are linked below.)
