## Summary

Representative Bethany Soye of District 9 explains HB 1080, the Help Not Harm bill, her only bill of the 2023 South Dakota session. It prohibits cross-sex hormones, puberty blockers and gender-transition surgeries for minors. She says it passed the House 60-10. She also talks about other states moving the other direction, and a South Dakota father who could not access his child's medical records.
