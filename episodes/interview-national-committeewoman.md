## Summary

Recorded in mid-February 2026, the week the House passed the SAVE America Act, just after an RNC meeting. Guest Heidi Engelhart, National Committeewoman for the South Dakota Republican Party (South Dakota's elected woman member of the Republican National Committee); she also writes and podcasts at Voice of the Plains "The Times, Places and Manner of holding Elections for Senators and Representatives, shall be prescribed in each State by the Legislature thereof; but the Congress may at any time by Law make or alter such Regulations, except as to the Places of choosing Senators." - Article I, Section 4 of the U.S. Constitution Update (October 2026) The midterm convention Engelhart previewed happened. The RNC held its first-ever midterm convention Sept. 9-10, 2026, at the American Airlines Center in Dallas, closing with speeches from Vice President JD Vance and President Trump. The SAVE America Act passed the House 218-213 on Feb. 11, 2026 (the Wednesday night she mentions), as a House amendment to S. 1383. Every Republican voted yes, joined by one Democrat, Rep. Henry Cuellar of Texas. South Dakota's Rep. Dusty Johnson voted yes. The Senate took it up in March and the filibuster stopped it, as she warned. On March 21 a cloture vote on a Republican amendment failed 49-41, and on March 26 cloture on Sen. Jon Husted's photo ID amendment failed 53-47, with all 53 Republicans voting yes and every Democrat and independent voting no. Sixty votes were needed. The bill has not passed the Senate. The original SAVE Act, H.R. 22, passed the House 220-208 on April 10, 2025, and has sat in the Senate since. The Make Elections Great Again Act, H.R. 7300 (Rep. Bryan Steil), has had no action since it was referred to committees in early February 2026.
