Show notes

Show notes

Heidi Engelhart, from Brown County, is South Dakota's Republican National Committeewoman, one of the state's three votes on the Republican National Committee along with the state chairman and the national committeeman. She won the post at the South Dakota GOP state convention in June 2024 after National Committeewoman Sandye Kading did not seek another term. She writes the Voice of the Plains Substack, where she posts RNC updates, party bylaws, platforms and resolutions, and her statewide prayer calls. This conversation was recorded in December 2024, before the RNC's January 2025 winter meeting and before President Trump's second inauguration. A correction: the RNC has four regions, not five. Engelhart's own Jan. 1, 2025 Voice of the Plains post lists them as Western, Midwestern, Northeastern and Southern. The 168 members are three from each of the 50 states, the District of Columbia and the five U.S. territories.

Update (Oct. 2026): At the January 2025 winter meeting, the RNC re-elected Michael Whatley as chairman and elected KC Crosbie of Kentucky, the treasurer Engelhart praises here, as co-chair in place of Lara Trump. Florida's Joe Gruters became treasurer. In August 2025, Whatley stepped down to run for the U.S. Senate in North Carolina with President Trump's endorsement, and the RNC elected Gruters as chairman at its summer meeting in Atlanta. The RNC later voted to hold a first-ever midterm convention, held in Dallas on Sept. 9 and 10, 2026, with President Trump billed as the headliner. Engelhart is still South Dakota's National Committeewoman, serving alongside National Committeeman Ried Holien and state Chairman Jim Eschenbaum. Two of the RNC prayer-call guests she mentions now serve in President Trump's Cabinet: Pete Hegseth at the Defense Department and Marco Rubio as secretary of state. Greg Blanc, whom she had scheduled to lead the devotion on her Jan. 7, 2025 prayer call, took his District 35 seat in the state Senate that January. (Sources are linked below.)
